# sidechain

One tape track ducks another. Pick a track, pick what it listens to (another track, or the beat), and it dips every time that source hits. Think of the kick pumping the bass.

## Getting there

- **Shift + mixer** opens the sidechain page. Shift + mixer again shows the stock signal-flow screen, and once more brings you back.
- **T1 to T4** pick the track that gets ducked. With 8 tracks on the unit, hold mixer and press T1 to T4 for tracks 5 to 8.

## Knobs

| Knob | Turn | Shift + turn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| blue | source: off, another track, or the beat | sensitivity: how loud a hit must be (a higher number needs a louder hit). With the beat as source: 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 or 1/16 |
| ochre | amount, 0 to 99 (99 ducks to silence) | attack, 0.5 to 50 ms |
| grey | hold, 5 ms to about 1 s | tone: what the duck listens for, 30 Hz to 8 kHz (the default hears a kick) |
| red | release, 10 ms to about 2 s | look-ahead, up to 20 ms |

- **Press blue**: bypass the duck on this track.
- **Press red**: change the release curve (out, in, linear, S).

## Good to know

- The settings are saved with the tape, for all eight tracks. A new or copied tape starts from the defaults.
- It only ducks while the tape plays. Stop, and every track comes back up.
- The source is a tape track or the beat, not a live synth or the input.
- Without 8 tracks it works on tracks 1 to 4. A source set to track 5 to 8 shows off until 8 tracks is back.
