# lfo

Four LFOs on every synth sound, all running at once. Each one is a stock LFO with its stock page, so you already know how they work. T4 shows them as four UFOs over a moon.

## Opening it

- **T4** on a synth sound opens the UFOs. On a drum sound, T4 stays stock.
- **T4** again opens the page of the LFO under the searchlight. T4 once more brings you back.
- T1 to T3 leave (T3 goes to the train, if you have fx train).

## Knobs on the UFOs

- **Turn**: pick the LFO's kind: none, tremolo, value, random, element, midi or velocity. A new one starts on the stock defaults.
- **Tap**: open that LFO's page. It takes the searchlight. On an empty UFO, the list of kinds opens instead.
- **Hold**: switch it off (the UFO lands) or back on.
- **Shift + turn**: move it one place. This only changes the order on screen: all four always run side by side.

## Each LFO's page

It's the stock page, knobs and all. On value, for example: blue sets the speed, ochre the amount, grey the destination, red the parameter.

With fx train on the unit and at least two wagons carrying an effect, the FX destination steps through the wagons, one per click. A small wagon in that knob's colour shows which one it moves, and the effect's parameter name shows over the knob.

## Presets

**Shift + T4** on the UFOs: tap for the list of saved sets, hold to save the one you're on. A new save takes the first free slot, named "lfo" and its number. On an LFO's own page, shift + T4 stays stock.

The list works like fx train's: blue scrolls, tap blue or press T4 to load, tap ochre to rename, tap grey to save, hold red to delete. Each row shows the set's four kinds in their knob colours.

Saved sets are files, 64 slots, in the `lfo` folder in disk mode.

## Good to know

- Each sound keeps its own four LFOs.
- Without the pack, T4 is stock again and the sound keeps one LFO: the one that was under the searchlight.
- Not tried on a unit yet: the midi kind's choice of wagon (it needs a controller), and a saved sound bringing its four LFOs back. That last one needs fx train on the unit too.
